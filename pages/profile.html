Data Management

What's stored, and where

OMS keeps everything — your assessment, Blueprints, builder projects, and Workbench — in this browser's local storage only. Nothing is sent to a server. Clearing your browser data, or opening OMS in a different browser or device, means starting fresh unless you've exported a copy below.

Actions

Export saves one JSON file with everything above, for backup or moving to another browser. Import overwrites the matching sections with what's in the file you choose. Both clear actions ask for confirmation first and cannot be undone.

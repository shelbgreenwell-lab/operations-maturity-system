Value Stream Intelligence

See how value moves, where work waits, and what constrains flow.

Organizations are structured vertically. Value usually moves horizontally. Customers experience the horizontal system — and most operational failure happens at waiting, handoffs, queues, approvals, rework, and information gaps, not inside any one team's own work.

USE THIS WHEN a customer outcome is slow or inconsistent and you need to see the whole flow, not just one team's part of it.

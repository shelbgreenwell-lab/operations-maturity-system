Capacity Intelligence

Understand whether demand, capacity, variability, or system design is constraining performance.

A queue is evidence of imbalance. It is not proof that headcount is the constraint. Capacity is not headcount — it is the amount of useful work the system can reliably produce.

USE THIS WHEN a backlog or overload looks like a staffing problem and you need to know what's actually consuming capacity.

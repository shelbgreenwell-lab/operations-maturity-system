KPI Architect

Design measures that support decisions, not dashboards that collect numbers.

Metrics without decisions are reporting, not management. Start from the decision a measurement needs to support, not from a list of numbers to track.

USE THIS WHEN you need to design or audit a set of KPIs and want to know whether each one actually earns its place.

To import candidate metrics directly, open a Value Stream or Capacity Model and look for "Add To Health Model" on its metrics.

Operating Debt

The cost of yesterday's workarounds.

Short-term operational workarounds accumulate into long-term cost. This tool draws candidate debt from findings already produced by Capacity, Blueprint, Governance, Risk, and Resilience — you decide what's actually worth tracking, what it costs to keep carrying, and who owns paying it down.

USE THIS WHEN a workaround has been "temporary" for long enough that it deserves to be named, owned, and prioritized.

Command Center already surfaces a rough approximation of operating debt from your assessment. A real register replaces that approximation with named, owned items.
